1. Editorial policies

Our comparison content aims to be clear, concise and practical. Key editorial principles include:

  • Using plain language and avoiding unnecessary jargon.
  • Highlighting material rates, fees and limitations where available.
  • Explaining how cards may fit different user profiles (e.g. students, travelers).
  • Updating content when providers change product terms or discontinue products.

2. Compliance with advertising and affiliate rules

The CreditCard Collection follows relevant guidance for affiliate and advertising disclosures, including requirements similar to those set by the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) in the United States and equivalent bodies in other jurisdictions.

Where affiliate relationships exist, they are disclosed through:

  • Site-wide disclosures on disclosure.creditcard.
  • Contextual disclosures near comparison tables or call-to-action buttons.

3. Data & privacy policies

We apply a data minimization approach. We operate without user accounts and do not collect sensitive financial details from visitors. Analytics and server logs are used to improve the service and maintain security, as described in the Privacy Policy.

4. Policy governance

All policies are overseen by ronarn AS as the operator of The CreditCard Collection. Policies may be revised to reflect changes in regulation, partner requirements or the structure of the network.

When significant changes are made, we will update the relevant policy pages and adjust the “last updated” dates. Users are encouraged to review these pages periodically.
